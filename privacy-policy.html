Privacy Policy

ReMe – Personal Reminders  ·  Last updated: June 11, 2026

ReMe does not collect, transmit, or share any personal data. Everything you enter stays on your device.

What data does ReMe store?

ReMe stores the following information locally on your device only:

  • Event titles, dates, times, and notes you enter
  • Locations you associate with events
  • Tags and visual themes you choose for each card
  • App preferences such as language and color scheme

This data never leaves your device and is not accessible to the developer or any third party.

Do you collect analytics or crash reports?

No. ReMe does not use any analytics SDK, tracking library, or crash reporting service. No usage data of any kind is sent anywhere.

Do you use advertising?

No. ReMe contains no advertisements and no advertising identifiers are accessed.

Location

ReMe may ask for location access to help you set a location for an event or show a map preview. Your location is used only within the app and is never stored on a server or shared with third parties.

Notifications

ReMe uses local notifications to remind you about upcoming events. These notifications are scheduled entirely on your device and do not involve any server or external service.

Siri & Shortcuts

ReMe supports App Intents so you can create events with Siri or the Shortcuts app. Data passed through these interactions is handled by Apple's frameworks and is not transmitted to the developer.

Children's Privacy

ReMe does not knowingly collect any information from anyone. Because no data is collected at all, the app is safe for users of all ages.

Changes to This Policy

If the privacy practices of the app change, this page will be updated with a new date. You are encouraged to review this page periodically.
