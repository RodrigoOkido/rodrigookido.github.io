Privacy Policy

Apps by Rodrigo Okido  ·  Covers ReMe and Keimy  ·  Last updated: September 22, 2026

Neither ReMe nor Keimy collect, transmit, or share any personal data. Everything you enter in either app stays on your device.

🗓 ReMe

Personal Reminders

What data does ReMe store?

ReMe stores the following information locally on your device only:

  • Event titles, dates, times, and notes you enter
  • Locations you associate with events
  • Tags and visual themes you choose for each card
  • App preferences such as language and color scheme

This data never leaves your device and is not accessible to the developer or any third party.

Location

ReMe may ask for location access to help you set a location for an event or show a map preview. Your location is used only within the app and is never stored on a server or shared with third parties.

Notifications

ReMe uses local notifications to remind you about upcoming events. These notifications are scheduled entirely on your device and do not involve any server or external service.

Siri & Shortcuts

ReMe supports App Intents so you can create events with Siri or the Shortcuts app. Data passed through these interactions is handled by Apple's frameworks and is not transmitted to the developer.

💰 Keimy

Personal Finance Tracker

What data does Keimy store?

Keimy stores the following information locally on your device only:

  • Your monthly income and fixed expenses
  • Expenses you log, along with their category and date
  • Piggy banks (savings goals) you create, including their target amount and deposit/withdrawal history
  • App preferences such as language, currency, and appearance

This data never leaves your device and is not accessible to the developer or any third party.

On-device Apple Intelligence

Keimy can generate a short, personalized read on your budget (a status summary and a few tips) using Apple's on-device Foundation Models framework, branded in the app as "Keimy." This processing happens entirely on your device — the figures used to generate it (like your income, expenses, and savings) are never sent to Apple's servers, the developer, or any third party.

Location, Notifications & Siri

Keimy does not request location access, does not send notifications, and does not integrate with Siri or Shortcuts.

Do you collect analytics or crash reports?

No. Neither app uses an analytics SDK, tracking library, or crash reporting service. No usage data of any kind is sent anywhere.

Do you use advertising?

No. Neither app contains advertisements, and no advertising identifiers are accessed.

Children's Privacy

Neither app knowingly collects any information from anyone. Because no data is collected at all, both apps are safe for users of all ages.

Changes to This Policy

If the privacy practices of either app change, this page will be updated with a new date. You are encouraged to review this page periodically.
